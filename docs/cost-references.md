# Cost and funding references

Start with the organisation that pays for each step of the service. Where delivery spans organisations, track who pays for the initial work, repeat work and follow-up. For each figure, record the source table or note, financial year, population, and whether the figure is actual spending, a budget, a forecast or an estimate. Keep capital and running costs identifiable. For a cost per person, case or completed job, match the costs and denominator to the same service, period and population.

## Where to look

- **Actual spending.** Use the department, agency, regulator or council accounts. Look for service expenditure, the accounting notes and audited outturn. Department-wide figures give context and often lack a breakdown by service. [HM Treasury spending framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/planning-and-performance-framework/the-governments-planning-and-performance-framework) · [Public Expenditure Statistical Analyses](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/public-expenditure-statistical-analyses-pesa)
- **Funding for changes.** Use Spending Review settlements, supply estimates, council budgets and grant determinations to check what is allocated, who can spend it and when. Trace whether a named programme is part of a broader allocation. [Supply Estimates guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/supply-estimates-guidance-manual)
- **Contracts.** Read award notices for scope, term, extensions and estimated value. Use accounts or payment records for what was spent. Compare figures on the same basis when checking reported savings. [Find a Tender](https://www.gov.uk/find-tender) · [Contracts Finder](https://www.gov.uk/contracts-finder)
- **Cost per case and policy effects.** Look for official unit costs, written parliamentary answers, audits, impact assessments and evaluations. Check which activities the estimate includes, its price year, and whether it is an average or the additional cost of a change. [NAO reports](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/) · [Written parliamentary questions](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/) · [Green Book appraisal guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2)

## Starting sources for social housing repairs in England

- **Private registered providers.** The Regulator of Social Housing’s quarterly survey gives recent repairs and maintenance spending and forecasts for the providers it covers. Its Global Accounts give annual expenditure and financing detail, including the treatment of capitalised major repairs. Check the coverage of each release before comparing totals. [Quarterly survey, July to September 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/quarterly-survey-for-q2-july-to-september-2026) · [2025 Global Accounts](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/2025-global-accounts-of-private-registered-providers/2025-global-accounts-of-private-registered-providers)
- **Council landlords.** Use Housing Revenue Accounts for income and expenditure relating to council-owned homes. Check the Housing Revenue Account lines in the housing services revenue outturn return (RO4) and the separate capital returns for major investment. Individual council accounts can provide more detail on repair budgets and who pays. [Housing Revenue Account guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/housing-revenue-account) · [Revenue outturn, 2025/26](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/local-authority-revenue-expenditure-and-financing-england-2025-to-2026-individual-local-authority-data-outturn) · [Capital outturn, 2025/26](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/local-authority-capital-expenditure-and-receipts-in-england-2025-to-2026-individual-local-authority-data)
- **Additional cost of Awaab’s Law.** The Phase 2 impact assessment estimates what the new requirements add to landlords’ existing responsibilities. Repairs that landlords were already required to make are part of its baseline. Use landlord accounts and actual performance evidence to check what happens after implementation. [Phase 2 final impact assessment](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/awaabs-law-phase-2-final-stage-impact-assessment)
