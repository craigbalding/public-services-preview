# Draft a public-service topic

Read the [editorial playbook](../../../docs/topic-playbook.md) and [topic format](../../../docs/topic-format.md) from this repository. Use the latest template for a new topic. For an update, work from the current topic, its template and its last reviewed evidence.

When starting from a [commissioned brief](../../../commissioning/README.md), confirm its status and then use the reader question, scope, starter records and drafting checks. Recheck current evidence before relying on the brief’s assessment. Record any material change to the framing or scope in the handoff.

For key figures, follow the playbook’s [source release check](../../../docs/topic-playbook.md#verify-the-publishers-release) before relying on an indexed copy.

1. Set the jurisdiction, population, subject and observation period. Write down the public promise. Use the question a reader brings as the title, leaving the answer open. Give the strongest answer the sources support in the opening summary, with its main qualification.
2. Trace the service from trigger to outcome. Identify what changes at each handoff and who owns the next step. Use rules or guidance to establish the intended process and audits or inspections to test how it operates. Attribute explanations for design choices to their sources.
3. Pick the findings that matter most. Connect each to its step and source. State the measure, the consequence and what would show progress. Use comparable observations and show where definitions, coverage or reporting change. Where cost helps explain delivery, check annual spending, funding for changes and costs through the service handoffs. [Cost and funding references](../../../docs/cost-references.md)
4. Check what studies establish about outcomes. Record any open question with the evidence needed to answer it. For scrutiny, record who asked, the official record, what the answer established and any dated commitment. Check whether each previously tracked item has new evidence.
5. Edit the topic and linked datasets. Keep source, actor, step, finding and follow-up IDs stable when updating the same item. Run `node src/build.mjs` and check the generated reading path, evidence links and expanded details.

Hand off the topic path, changed claims, source references, open evidence gaps and build result. For an update, identify what changed since the previous review and what each change in status means for the reader. Follow the publication scope of the current request.
